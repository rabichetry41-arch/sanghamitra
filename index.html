Our Story

From Women SHGs to a Proud FPC

Sanghamitra Mahila Producer Company Ltd. is a women-led Farmer Producer Company built from dozens of Self-Help Groups across Bijni. Every litre of milk is collected, tested and processed by the same women who raise the herds — so the income, and the decisions, stay in their hands.
